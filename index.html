A LITTLE CONTEXT

I like knowing what happens underneath.

I'm a software developer who moves between product interfaces and the systems beneath them. Some days that means shaping an AI workspace or a browser-based 3D tool. Other days it means working through boot code, memory maps, and the details a framework usually hides.

I care about clear architecture, understandable interactions, useful documentation, and reliable builds. I prefer shipping a small working slice, learning from it, and improving it over presenting a concept as if it were already finished.

My public work is the best record of what I'm learning and building now.
